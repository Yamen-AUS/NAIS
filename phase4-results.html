AP Course Pass Rates & Results

College Board AP examinations measure university-level mastery. A score of 3 or above is considered a passing score and earns college credit at most universities. Grades 10–12 participate.

AP Scoring Scale: Scores range from 1–5. A score of 3 = Qualified, 4 = Well Qualified, and 5 = Extremely Well Qualified. Most universities grant credit for scores of 3 and above.
