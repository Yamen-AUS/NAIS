Our Approach

The American Curriculum
Advantage

NAIS delivers the rigorous American curriculum — the same standard used by top schools across the United States — enhanced by our international community context and Dubai's global perspective. Our programs are designed to nurture whole-child development: academic excellence, critical thinking, creativity, and strong character.

From Kindergarten through Grade 12, students progress through carefully sequenced programs that build on each other, ensuring a seamless educational journey that prepares them for the world's top universities.

KG 1 & KG 2

Our Kindergarten program creates a warm, joyful learning environment where young children develop a love of discovery and build the foundational skills they'll carry through their entire education.

Through play-based learning, hands-on exploration, and nurturing teacher relationships, our youngest students develop literacy, numeracy, social skills, creativity, and confidence in a safe and stimulating classroom environment.

Our High School program is a college-preparatory experience of the highest standard. Students are challenged with rigorous coursework, including Advanced Placement (AP) courses, while being supported by expert teachers and dedicated university counselors.

Every Child Belongs

Inclusion
Program

NAIS is deeply committed to inclusive education. Our dedicated Inclusion Program ensures that students with diverse learning needs receive the individualized support, accommodations, and expertise needed to thrive academically and socially.

Our trained Special Education professionals work collaboratively with classroom teachers to develop personalized learning plans (IEPs), implement evidence-based strategies, and create an environment where every student belongs and succeeds.

STEM Excellence

Math & Sciences

Our STEM program is at the heart of the NAIS academic experience. Students work in state-of-the-art laboratories and participate in hands-on, inquiry-based learning that develops genuine scientific thinking and mathematical reasoning.

From early numeracy in Kindergarten to AP Calculus and AP Physics in Grade 12, every student builds a strong foundation in mathematical thinking, scientific inquiry, and data literacy.
